How We Work

The Terrafirma process.

Every installation begins with deep listening. We understand your budget, timeline, code requirements, and aesthetic vision before a single chip is poured.

Step A

Site Assessment

We evaluate substrate condition, load requirements, moisture levels, and existing floor systems. No guesswork — just data.

Step B

Design Selection

Choose from hundreds of chip varieties, binders, and divider strip metals. We produce physical samples before any commitment.

Step C

Preparation

Substrate grinding, crack repair, and primer application. The floor beneath the floor is as important as what's on top.

Step D

Pour & Cure

Matrix is poured, leveled, and allowed to cure fully — no shortcuts. Rushing this step is how you get a floor that fails in five years.

Step E

Grinding & Polish

Sequential diamond grinding reveals the aggregate pattern. Final polish brings the floor to its specified sheen — from matte to mirror.

What We Use

Material integrity,
without compromise.

We work exclusively with Portland cement and epoxy binders from established suppliers. Our aggregate library includes marble, glass, granite, mother-of-pearl, and recycled post-industrial chips — all verified for consistency and sourced responsibly.

Divider strips in brass, aluminum, or white alloy define patterns and control expansion. These aren't decorative afterthoughts — they are structural elements that prevent cracking and allow the floor to breathe over decades of thermal cycling.

We carry no proprietary product lines. Every specification we make is driven by what performs best for your space, not what's most profitable for us.
